Tidepool Tussle — a duel of the tidepools. You are the Guardian of the Pools, duelling Pell, King of the Tidepools, with a deck of 32 sea creatures across four families: Wave, Shell, Coral and Kelp. Spend bubbles to summon creatures, attack Pell's creatures or Pell himself, and drain his Tide from 20 to 0 before he drains yours. Two hero powers per side, once per game each: your Tide Surge (+2 bubbles, +2 Tide) and Reef Wall (heal all your creatures to full), against Pell's Royal Blessing (restores 5 Tide the first time his drops to 12) and Deep Current (draws 2 cards the first time he starts a turn with an empty board and almost nothing in hand). A tidepool terrain draws one of four effects every round — High Tide (+1 attack to all Wave), Calm Water (every creature heals 1), Kelp Bloom (+1 health to all Kelp), Storm Surge (every creature takes 1 damage) — shown in a banner with the next effect forecast a round ahead, and every card shows a hover tooltip explaining its keyword and family. A complete 1v1 card battler that plays offline in a desktop browser: title screen with rules, a full game loop with an AI opponent, and win and loss screens. Round 3 adds 8 more creatures (32 total, deck 36) and the second hero power for each side; a 360-game simulation of the greedy player policy against Pell's AI comes out at 48.1% player wins with no deadlocks.
